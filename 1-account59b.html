Who can talk in this app

Creator-only is a courtesy gate in this app, not secure authorization. Modified clients can bypass it.

Local expiry

Expiry hides/stops the channel in this app. It does not delete server metadata, recordings, or other peers' copies/access.

A password changes the secret peer-channel address. The password itself is not stored. Anyone given the secret address could connect, so share it carefully. Each new channel has a random ID. Before you join

1. Choose a channel or create your own.
2. Hold TALK to speak, let go to listen.
3. Allow the mic to talk, or choose Listen only.

Listeners can save voice

Incoming transmissions are saved locally for replay, up to 20 per channel on each listener's device. Nothing is uploaded as a recording. Busy mode mutes live voice and keeps a listen-later queue while connected. You can turn saving off and delete your copies, but cannot delete copies on other devices. Do not share sensitive information.

Recordings are limited to 60 seconds each. Browser storage may be cleared or fail. Legacy clients are not recorded by this version.

Enable your microphone

So your crew can hear you while you hold TALK. Listeners may save incoming voice locally for replay. No recordings are uploaded. Your microphone stays muted between transmissions and is released when this app goes into the background. Reopen a channel when you return. Your browser will ask next.
